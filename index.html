Moon Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

About Moon

Moon is a bot for Root communities designed to welcome members when they join.

Information Used

Moon may process member and community identifiers and information made available by Root that is necessary to detect when a member joins and send a welcome message.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the bot's welcome feature, maintain its functionality, and troubleshoot issues. Moon is not intended to sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Storage and Sharing

The information stored, its retention period, and any sharing depend on the bot's implementation and hosting. We will update this policy if Moon's data practices change.

Third-Party Services

Moon operates on the Root platform. Root's own services are subject to Root's applicable policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

Moon is a community utility, not a service designed to collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Moon's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published here.
